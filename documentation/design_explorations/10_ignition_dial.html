Strengths

The ignition metaphor teaches itself — every learner already knows what turning a key does, so OFF→CHECK→START needs no label. Setup, plate status and the start action share one object instead of competing for space, and the recording cluster reuses the same face instead of switching screens.

Weaknesses

A twist gesture on flat glass has no physical detents to catch a thumb on, so the stop points are easy to overshoot or miss; the design also needs an accessible tap-to-advance fallback, and dense gauge text will strain low-vision users.

When this design wins

Demos and brand moments where the car metaphor itself is the pitch, more than the fastest possible start.
