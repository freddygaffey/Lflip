Try it
Live rebuild of the flow above — wait for plates to connect, tap the circle to start, watch it become the timer, then Stop or Cancel to return.

Strengths

One unmistakable action, sized for a thumb in a parked car; state is legible at arm’s length because the whole circle changes colour, and setup collapses to a four-tile glance.

Weaknesses

The Guest-driver flow hides behind a tile tap, long car or supervisor names cramp the tiles, and the giant circle wastes vertical space once a trip is already running.

When this design wins

Quick repeat trips — same car, same supervisor — where the learner just wants to hit go and drop the phone in the cradle.
