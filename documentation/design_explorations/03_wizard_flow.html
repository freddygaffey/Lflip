Strengths

Nobody can miss a required field — each step validates before Next unlocks. One question per screen keeps targets big and copy calm, which suits a nervous first-timer sitting in the driveway.

Weaknesses

Repeat users pay a tap tax every single drive: four screens to pick the same car and the same supervisor. The stepper is pure chrome once the flow is familiar.

When this design wins

First weeks with the app, shared family phones, and anywhere a skipped odometer reading would quietly corrupt the logbook.
