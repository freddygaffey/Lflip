Live prototype
Drag the thumb — the mechanism this whole exploration is about.

Strengths

The slide gesture makes starting a drive feel deliberate and prevents pocket-starts; plate state lives in the track colour, so one glance answers “can I go?”. The drum odometer invites correction instead of demanding typed input, and carousels make the common car/supervisor pair a zero-tap default.

Weaknesses

Drag targets are hostile to switch-access and screen-reader users without a parallel tap path. The drum is slow when the real odometer differs by hundreds of km, and hidden carousel items make a fourth car easy to miss.

When this design wins

One-handed use in a parked car, where accidental starts are costly and the same two people share every drive.
