04 · Rejected variant — tall sheet, full formLost: the full-height form buries the map that justifies this direction, and Start sinks below six fields.

Strengths

Recording state is instantly legible — the route grows on the map and the elapsed pill reads at a glance. Chips keep the common case to two taps before Start.

Weaknesses

Before a trip starts the map is pure decoration, and keeping it live costs GPS and battery. Chips make small tap targets, and the guest-supervisor path hides behind a hint line.

When this design wins

Learners and parents who care where the hours happened, and live demos — a moving map sells the product faster than any form could.
