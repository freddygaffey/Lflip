Strengths

Opening the app answers "how far along am I?" instantly; the dominant total ring makes every drive feel like visible progress, which nudges reluctant learners to start one.

Weaknesses

The core task — starting a trip — sits below the charts, so frequent users scroll past decoration daily. The hero also duplicates the Dashboard tab's job.

When this design wins

Early in the 100 hours, when motivation matters more than speed; weaker once logging is habitual and the user just wants the green button.
