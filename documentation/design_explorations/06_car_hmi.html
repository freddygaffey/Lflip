L Flip — design exploration 06 · Car-dashboard HMI

Log Trip built for a phone in a windscreen cradle: instrument-cluster tiles, glanceable type, brand colours carrying state, and one full-width start bar you can hit without looking.

Interactive — try it

Plates pair automatically on load. Once the bar turns green, start the trip and watch the timer run — stop it whenever you like.

Strengths

Everything reads at arm’s length from a cradle; one thumb starts or stops a trip without looking; brand colours carry state (green go, amber pairing, red stop) so the screen is scannable in peripheral vision.

Weaknesses

Heavy-handed for on-the-couch review — four giant tiles to say four small facts. Dark data entry (guest name, licence number) is harder, and the huge timer wastes space once you stop glancing at it.

When this design wins

Phone mounted on the windscreen, engine idling: pre-drive checks, one confident start, mid-drive stop. Filled elevated cards beat hairline outlines — they survive glare and stay separable at 25% scale.
