Strengths

Zero learning curve — the ledger is the ACT paper logbook every learner and supervisor already knows. Instant credibility with parents and assessors; the new trip as "the next row" makes the mental model obvious.

Weaknesses

Six columns are cramped on 390 px; cells are too small to be touch targets, so editing must happen elsewhere. Nostalgia fights usability — the skeuomorph variant proved texture and handwriting cost more legibility than they buy trust.

When this design wins

When the audience is supervisors and assessors reviewing history, or in an evidence/export view. Less suited as the primary start-trip surface, where one big action beats a dense table.
