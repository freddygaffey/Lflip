Live prototype
Every switch, the guard and the master throw are live.

Strengths

The guard makes the point of no return physically unmissable — you cannot arm a drive by accident, and the annunciator strip answers “is everything set?” at a glance without reading text. Aviation framing gives four unrelated preconditions (car, supervisor, odometer, plates) one consistent, learnable gesture.

Weaknesses

Four switches plus a guard is more taps than a single button, and first-time users may not guess a switch throws itself for BLE. Toggle affordances are poor for screen readers and low-motor-control users without a parallel control.

When this design wins

Supervised early lessons, where the ritual of checking each precondition builds the habit and the guard’s friction is a feature, not a cost.
