Decision

Big Button + grid is the shipped design (27/30). It ties the Car HMI on every in-car criterion but stays comfortable off the cradle, and is the cheapest to reach from the existing Ionic screen. The Car HMI (26/30) loses only where its own evaluation admits: it is heavy-handed the moment the phone leaves the windscreen.

The losing directions still contribute: the HMI’s tile discipline is the grid under the button; the wizard’s field-gating survives as the start button refusing to enable until odometer and supervisor are valid; the dashboard rings stay on the Dashboard tab; the ledger is the right shape for the history / evidence view; the map earns its place after the trip, in the trip-detail route view.

Colour remains a live decision. The exploration favoured green on brand grounds (red is reserved for Stop and errors); the red record-convention build is preserved as evidence. If red is chosen, Stop must be differentiated by shape and position so two red actions cannot be confused.

Method: six parallel builds from one shared brief, none seeing the others’ work; every prototype self-verified by rendering and correcting its own screenshot; weaknesses recorded by each direction’s own author before comparison.
